How ordering works

  1. Add books to your cart, or press Buy now. Stripe's secure checkout opens in a new tab.
  2. Pick how many copies, enter your shipping address and phone number, and pay by card.
  3. You get a receipt by email. Classics Library buyers also get a sample copy by email.
  4. Our team contacts you about shipping. Shipping is billed separately, and expedited shipping is available.
