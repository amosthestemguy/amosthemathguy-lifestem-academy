# The Classics Library: signature content

The Classics Library is Counting to Calculus's signature offering. Feature it every week.

## Facts (checked against the covers collage)
- Four volumes for Grades 5–12: **116 companion editions** and **1,319 lessons**.
- Vol I · Ancient Math History, from Babylon and Egypt to AD 476: 15 editions, 181 lessons, Grades 5 and 9.
- Vol II · Middle Ages Math History, AD 476 to 1476: 17 editions, 175 lessons, Grades 6 and 10.
- Vol III · Early Modern Math History, 1476 to 1776: 43 editions, 545 lessons, Grades 7 and 11.
- Vol IV · Science and Technology History, 1776 to 2026: 41 editions, 418 lessons, Grades 8 and 12.
- Every edition follows the same three steps: read their lives, read their words, work their mathematics.
