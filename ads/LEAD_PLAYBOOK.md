# Counting to Calculus: Lead Playbook

The path to 100 students: **ad or post → Free Math Readiness Check → free live trial class → enrollment.**

---

## 1. Meta Instant Form ("Free Math Readiness Check")

Your tinyurl link opens a Google Form. Meta can't see sign-ups on a Google Form, so it can't learn which parents are converting. An Instant Form fixes that: the parent fills it in inside Facebook or Instagram, pre-filled with their name and email.

**Set it up in Ads Manager (about 10 minutes):**
1. Clear Meta's login verification first (see step 3 of the launch checklist below).
2. Go to Ads Manager → **Create** → **Leads** → Instant forms.
3. In the ad set, choose the **Counting To Calculus** Page, United States, ages 25–55, a $15–30/day budget, and Advantage+ placements.
4. Make 4 ads, one per video (Meet Amos, Close the Gaps, Pick Your Time, Small Group). Paste the text and headline for each from `AD_COPY.md`. Set the button to **Sign Up**.
5. Build the form (create it once and reuse it on all 4 ads):

| Section | Content |
|---|---|
| Form type | **Higher intent**: parents confirm their answers before submitting, which means fewer junk leads |
| Intro headline | Free Math Readiness Check |
| Intro text | Find out exactly where your child is in math, and get a free live trial class. Taught live online by certified tutors, with classes designed by Amos Tarfa (AmosTheMathGuy). |
| Question 1 | Child's grade (multiple choice): 3rd–4th, 5th–6th, 7th–8th, 9th–10th, 11th–12th |
| Question 2 | Main subject (multiple choice): Math, English, Math + Science |
| Question 3 | Best time for a free trial class (multiple choice): Mon–Thu 5 PM ET, 6 PM ET, 7 PM ET, 8 PM ET, Saturday 12–3 PM ET |
| Contact fields | Parent's full name, email, phone number (all pre-filled by Meta) |
| Privacy policy | Link to your privacy policy page (required). Collect only the parent's details plus the child's grade, never the child's name or contact details. |
| Completion screen | "Thank you! Amos or a tutor will reach out within 24 hours to book [Child]'s free trial class." Button: **Visit website** → tinyurl.com/CountingToCalculus |

6. **Get leads by email:** go to **Page → Meta Business Suite → All tools → Instant Forms → CRM setup**, and turn on email notifications to your address. The daily check-in already searches your Gmail and adds new leads to the dashboard.

**Can I build this for you?** Not yet. adplane can create the campaign, ad sets and videos, but it can't attach an Instant Form to an ad, so the form step has to happen in Ads Manager. Once the form exists, I can watch the results every day.

## 3. Text-message reminders (send from your phone or WhatsApp; parent must have given you their number)

- **When booked:** "Hi [Name], this is Amos from Counting to Calculus. [Child]'s free trial class is booked for [day] at [time] ET. Here's the link to join: [link]. Reply here with any questions!"
- **24 hours before:** "Hi [Name]! Reminder: [Child]'s free math class is tomorrow at [time] ET. Link: [link]. See you there."
- **1 hour before:** "We start in 1 hour! [link]. [Child] will need a notebook and pencil."
- **Within 2 hours after the trial:** "Thank you for joining today! [One specific thing Child did well]. I'd love to keep working with [Child]. Live Class is $300/mo, or Small Group is $750/mo. Want me to hold a seat?"
- **No-show:** "We missed [Child] today! No problem. Want to pick another time? Mon–Thu 5–8 PM ET or Sat 12–3 PM ET."

## 4. Ten new ad hooks (the opening line of a video or post)

Each one speaks to a parent's real worry, then points to the solution.

1. "If your 7th grader freezes at fractions, here's the missing step."
2. "Top of the class? That's where we start."
3. "Your child doesn't need more homework. They need a coach."
4. "Winter break erases math. Our students keep learning, live."
5. "Bored in math class is a warning sign, not a compliment."
6. "Abraham Lincoln studied Euclid. Why don't our kids?"
7. "Calculus shouldn't be optional. Here's how every child gets there."
8. "Is your child ready for high school math? Find out free."
9. "4–6 students. One tutor who knows your child's name and their gaps."
10. "Using an ESA or homeschooling? Live math, from counting to calculus."

**How to use them:** film each hook as the first 3 seconds of a whiteboard or on-camera clip, then answer it in 30–40 seconds. The daily check-in will suggest one per day.
