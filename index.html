The school day

10:00 a.m. – 3:00 p.m. Eastern

Math and science every day, with an integrated afternoon that ties them together and adds logic, history, and the philosophy of science.

10:00 – 11:30
Math Review. Core math on the Counting to Calculus sequence — warm-up, worked examples, then small-group practice.
11:30 – 1:00
Science. The day's concept with a demonstration or lab, tied to the scientists who discovered it.
1:00 – 1:30
Lunch & movement. A real break — food, rest, and a chance to move.
1:30 – 3:00
Applied STEM & Great Thinkers. Math + science together: labs, projects, plus logic, philosophy of science, and a great-thinker study.

Two ways to join — tuition & aid

Premium, live, small-group instruction — priced accessibly. Most families will not pay full price: need-based flexible tuition and scholarships are available, and no family is turned away for cost alone.

Full Academy · Mon–Thu

Saturday Cohort

$3,900/year  ·  or $390/mo ×10
  • One full 10–3 ET STEM day, Saturdays
  • Includes Parent University for parents
  • Great alongside other schooling

Financial aid & scholarships. Need-based flexible tuition brings the Full Academy as low as $3,900/year and the Saturday Cohort to $1,500/year for families with the highest need. Merit and access scholarships — the Founder's Scholarship (50%), STEM Excellence Scholarship (25%), and Legacy of Learning grant (15%) — are available in limited numbers. Sibling (10%), pay-in-full (5%), and early-bird (5%) discounts also apply.

Tuition is benchmarked to leading live, small-class online schools (2026): mid-range online private runs $10–11k/year and premium live programs $12–20k+/year — Lifestem is positioned at the accessible end of the premium tier.
